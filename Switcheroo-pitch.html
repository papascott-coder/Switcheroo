Switcheroo

A card for two people who are standing there together. One person has something they do not want. The other has something they do. Or something just changed hands — keys, cash, a finished job — and both of you want proof. The card holds both names, so “we’re good” is not a text that disappears.

How it works

  1. Open Switcheroo.
  2. Pick one job: Stamp this, or Start a Swap.
  3. Name the thing. For a swap, also name what you want.
  4. The other person is right there. Bump phones.
  5. Both stamp it. On Seeker that stamp is a fingerprint in the phone’s vault. Keys never leave the phone.
  6. The card is done. Both names are on it.

If two kids want to trade toys, the grown-ups stamp. The kids never get wallets, and they never hold tokens.

What it can do for you

  • You never start an argument over what was agreed upon. The card is still there in November.
  • You can turn a thing in the garage, the closet, or the toy bin into something you actually want.
  • You do not finish the trade by keeping your lamp. You finish it when they get the lamp and you get the chair.
  • You can hold a little SKR in the middle until both of you say settled. That part is optional.
  • The person who shows up and stamps can earn a small thank-you.

One place, one time

You do not text back and forth, then write it down, then try to remember who said what. The two of you do it once, in one app, while you are both there.
