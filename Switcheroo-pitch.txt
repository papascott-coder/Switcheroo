Switcheroo

What it is

Switcheroo is a card for two people who are standing there together. One person has something they do not want. The other has something they do. Or something just changed hands — keys, cash, a finished job — and both of you want proof. The card holds both names, so “we’re good” is not a text that disappears.

How it works

1. You open Switcheroo.
2. You pick one job: Stamp this (we both agree this happened) or Start a Swap (my leftover for yours).
3. You name the thing. For a swap, you also name what you want.
4. The other person is right there. You bump phones, or tap their name in this practice version.
5. You both stamp it. On a Seeker phone that stamp is a fingerprint in the phone’s vault. Keys never leave the phone.
6. The card is done. Both names are on it. You can look it up later.

If two kids want to trade toys, the grown-ups stamp. The kids never get wallets, and they never hold tokens.

What it can do for you

- You never start an argument over what was agreed upon. The card is still there in November.
- You can turn a thing in the garage, the closet, or the toy bin into something you actually want.
- You do not finish the trade by keeping your lamp. You finish it when they get the lamp and you get the chair. Both of you walk away with what you wanted.
- You can hold a little SKR in the middle, like a polite deposit, until both of you say settled. That part is optional.
- The person who shows up and stamps the other side can earn a small thank-you. Helping them get what they want is how the deal closes.
- A car handoff, a paid-in-full sale, or a job that is actually finished can be stamped the same way. One kind of card covers the handshake and the trade.

One place, one time

You do not text back and forth, then write it down, then try to remember who said what. The two of you do it once, in one app, while you are both there. Pick the job, name the thing, stamp it, and you are done.
